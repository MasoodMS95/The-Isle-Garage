# Released Evrima species catalog

Checked 2026-09-06. User-provided in-game species selector screenshots, relayed by ProjectManagerGPT, independently confirm the exact 22 names and diet groups below; overlapping screenshots were deduplicated. The selector and bundled artwork cover 22 released playables. Deinosuchus and Pteranodon are included as playable animals even though they are not dinosaurs. Existing stored species names are not rewritten. T-Rex/T rex/Trex and Pachy resolve to the corresponding illustration only; custom and unknown species retain their names with a neutral fallback.

| Diet | Playables with bundled original SVG illustrations |
| --- | --- |
| Carnivore | Allosaurus, Austroraptor, Carnotaurus, Ceratosaurus, Deinosuchus, Dilophosaurus, Herrerasaurus, Omniraptor, Pteranodon, Troodon, Tyrannosaurus |
| Herbivore | Diabloceratops, Dryosaurus, Hypsilophodon, Kentrosaurus, Maiasaura, Pachycephalosaurus, Stegosaurus, Tenontosaurus, Triceratops |
| Omnivore | Beipiaosaurus, Gallimimus |

The inventory is cross-checked against the fan-maintained Evrima Quick Guide pages, each marked updated September 4, 2026: [carnivores](https://www.evrimaquickguide.com/playables/quick-facts-carnivores), [herbivores](https://www.evrimaquickguide.com/playables/quick-facts-herbivores), [omnivores](https://www.evrimaquickguide.com/playables/quick-facts-omnivores). These are community references, not official guarantees of server availability.

[Official Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/376210/announcements/) confirm Austroraptor entered the public branch in patch 0.21.772 on August 3, 2026 and mention Kentrosaurus fixes. The August 31 DevBlog #72 describes Oviraptor/Baryonyx work toward QA/Hordetest; they are excluded from this released roster, as are other announced/test-only animals. Recheck release notes before expanding the list. No guide or game artwork was copied.

The silhouettes communicate species identity only; actual skin codes, growth, status and Prime remain separate record fields. A missing/custom species shows the fallback in the garage, public profile and OG preview. No user-uploaded or remote image URL participates in rendering.
